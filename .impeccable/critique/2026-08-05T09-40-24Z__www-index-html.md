## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Grounded, not generic, with one real gap. The exercise copy is genuinely coach-voiced and situational ("You just made a mistake... What happens in the next ten seconds decides the rest of your performance"), and the Flow Overlay executes DESIGN.md's Quiet Compass metaphor literally: a gold orb pulsing inside a full-bleed teal radial gradient, no card chrome. Mood-triggered branching (low mood → breathing exercise, high mood → mastery log) is a rare case of UI logic actually enacting the coaching mechanism, not just displaying content about it. The gap: Home's composition itself (ring stat → mood → hero recommendation → quick breath → ritual → goal → reframe) is a conventional stacked-card wellness dashboard any streaks-and-mood app could ship unchanged. The words and the Flow Overlay are Keelstone; the dashboard shape is generic scaffolding.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` returned exit code 2, 61 findings against `www/index.html`. 5 warnings: a `side-tab` accent border, two `bounce-easing` curves (splash rings, yoga-done animation), a `layout-transition`, and the `.orb`'s `dark-glow` box-shadow. 56 advisory: 49 font-size values and 4 radius values falling outside the exact steps `DESIGN.md` documents (1.45/1.05/0.92/0.76rem and 8/10/12/16/999px), plus 3 colors (`rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`, `#b9503c` ×2) with no token. Cross-checked against `DESIGN.md`: **no false positives**, this is real, extensive drift from the design system written this session, a finding the design-review pass did not surface because it wasn't auditing literal token values.

**Visual overlays**: Live browser injection on the Profile screen (dark theme) surfaced 22 additional anti-patterns not visible from source alone: **10 low-contrast** violations, most seriously white text on the gold "Opprett konto" button at **1.8:1** and white-on-teal Pro banner text at **2.8:1** (WCAG AA floor is 4.5:1), plus a segmented control at 2.1:1 that reads as disabled when it isn't. **4 undersized-ui-text** hits: the bottom nav labels render at 10.88px, under the 11px floor. **6 nested-cards**, corroborating the design-review's Home-density finding. Screenshots of all four tabs plus an annotated Profile overlay were captured; the browser session and both background servers were confirmed stopped.

## Overall Impression

The product has a real point of view where it counts, the exercise scripts and the Flow Overlay, and loses it where a stressed user actually lands first: Home. The single biggest opportunity is collapsing Home to the one-action-at-a-time discipline the product's own copy and Product Principles already commit to, while fixing a set of concrete, detector-confirmed accessibility failures (contrast, undersized nav text) that would otherwise undercut the "instrument you can trust under pressure" positioning on day one.

## What's Working

1. **The Flow Overlay** (breathing/yoga/ritual full-screen takeover) is the strongest thing in the codebase: real eased orb/ring physics, a live SVG progress ring, a physiological safety note. It's the one place the Quiet Compass metaphor is fully realized, not just described.
2. **Mood-triggered branching** operationalizes the actual coaching mechanism: a low mood tap routes straight into a breathing exercise, a high mood tap opens the mastery log. This is UI logic doing the product's actual job, not just displaying content about it.
3. **Coach-authored copy** ("Pressure isn't your enemy. Pressure is a sign that something matters to you") is specific and embodied enough that the exercise screen alone could not be mistaken for a generic mindfulness app.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Home violates the product's own "one concrete action" principle and the Rare Gold Rule**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md Principle 1 states "one concrete action per exercise, not general advice." Home presents 7 parallel decision points with 2+ gold CTAs visible simultaneously, contradicting DESIGN.md's own Rare Gold Rule ("ideally one per screen") and confirmed independently by the detector's 6 nested-card findings. For a user opening the app minutes before a competition or meeting, this is a dashboard to parse, not a compass to consult.
- **Fix**: Collapse Home to a single primary CTA (the recommended exercise, or the mood-driven branch); move ritual, goal, and reframe behind a secondary "More tools" disclosure or into Explore/Profile.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Multiple WCAG AA contrast failures, most severely on the primary "Create account" CTA (1.8:1)**
- **Why it matters**: Detector-confirmed via live DOM injection, not a false positive: white text on the gold button reads at 1.8:1, the Pro banner at 2.8:1, both well under the 4.5:1 floor. On the exact screen (Profile → account creation) where the product is asking a user to trust it with an email and password, the CTA's own label is close to unreadable for a meaningful share of users.
- **Fix**: Use `--blue-d` or a darker ink color for text on Compass Gold surfaces instead of white; re-check the Pro banner's white-on-teal-light combination against the 4.5:1 floor.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] A broken, non-functional native audio player is shown on every exercise**
- **Why it matters**: `renderExerciseDetail()` renders a live `<audio controls>` element pointing at a file that PRODUCT.md confirms doesn't exist yet, directly beside a banner already saying "Audio version coming soon." A stressed user who taps the visible native play button gets silence with zero feedback, at the exact moment the product exists to be trustworthy.
- **Fix**: Don't render the `<audio>` element until a file actually exists for that exercise id/language; the fallback banner alone communicates the state correctly.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Design-system token drift: 61 detector findings against the DESIGN.md written this session**
- **Why it matters**: 49 font-size and 4 radius values in the live CSS fall outside the exact scale steps DESIGN.md now documents as canonical, plus 3 untokenized colors. This is exactly the drift `/impeccable document` exists to prevent going forward; left alone, DESIGN.md will read as aspirational rather than descriptive within a few more edits.
- **Fix**: Sweep the flagged lines and either fold each value into the nearest documented step or add a deliberate new step to DESIGN.md's scale where the value is genuinely a new, reusable size.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable extract`

**[P2] Inconsistent destructive-action guardrails**
- **Why it matters**: Account deletion has a translated confirm dialog; deleting a ritual mantra or a process goal, arguably more personally invested content, is a single click with zero recovery path.
- **Fix**: Add the same lightweight confirm pattern already built for account deletion to `deleteRitual()`/`deleteGoal()`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**, opening the app 5 minutes before a meeting: Home's 7-card stack means the thumb has to scroll past the mood row and hero card before reaching content that isn't the algorithmic recommendation; the broken `<audio>` control is exactly the kind of element a fast, low-patience tap will hit and get nothing back from. State persistence on interruption (localStorage on every change) is a genuine strength for this persona.

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**, walking onboarding into the first exercise: the 4 category choices (Focus/Calm/Confidence/Restitution) offer icon + label only, no explanation of what each contains before commitment; landing on Home immediately after presents the same 7-card wall with no spotlight on a single next step; the favorite and overlay-close buttons carry no visible label for a persona defined by taking the most literal interpretation of any given label, and here there is none to interpret.

**Marcus (project-specific: the Leader/Professional audience from PRODUCT.md)**, opening the app before a board meeting on a work phone: the mood-emoji row sits above the fold on Home, ahead of the actual recommended exercise, reading as consumer-wellness rather than "instrument you consult" for this specific audience, the opposite of DESIGN.md's stated positioning. Home currently offers no visual distinction between "I have 5 minutes before something happens now" and "I want to reflect on my week," despite PRODUCT.md's situational-first principle implying those should be different entry points.

## Minor Observations

- Bottom nav labels render at 10.88px, under the 11px floor (detector-confirmed).
- The raw-JSON `<textarea>` manual-backup UI is a jarring technical register shift, most likely to intimidate the non-technical users still most likely to be anonymous.
- The header streak badge shows "0" prominently for every brand-new user before they've done anything.
- The disclaimer and support email are bundled into the lowest-priority card on Profile, easy to read as boilerplate for a mental-health-adjacent product.
- Two more `bounce-easing`/`layout-transition`/`dark-glow` instances (splash sequence, yoga-done card) are low-severity but easy same-pass fixes alongside the token-drift sweep.
